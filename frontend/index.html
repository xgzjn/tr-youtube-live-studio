Quick Setup

  1. Upload a video (Video Upload page) — MP4 / MKV / MOV.
  2. Stream Settings — paste YouTube RTMP URL + Stream Key, choose 720p / 30fps / 2500 kbps, duration 24h.
  3. Click START LIVE. Confirm in YouTube Studio → Go Live.
  4. Your PC can close — the VPS keeps looping the video for 24 hours.
